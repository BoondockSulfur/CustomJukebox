Put your music here, as OGG Vorbis files.

This example expects three of them, matching the discs CustomJukebox creates
in disc.json on first start:

    epic_journey.ogg
    ocean_dreams.ogg
    forest_walk.ogg

Convert with ffmpeg:

    ffmpeg -i input.mp3 -c:a libvorbis -q:a 5 epic_journey.ogg

Then delete this file. See the README in the root of this pack for the rest.
